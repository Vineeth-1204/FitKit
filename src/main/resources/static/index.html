Nutrition Note: Raw vs. Cooked Tracking Yield Principle

Grains and legumes absorb 1.5x to 3x their weight in water during boiling. 100g of raw white rice (365 kcal) expands into 280g of cooked rice (130 kcal/100g). If you consume 200g of cooked rice, always select Rice, white, cooked (260 kcal) instead of raw grains to prevent inadvertent calorie overestimation.
